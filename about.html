About Loona Speaking Book

Loona Speaking Book · Loona Automation

Loona Speaking Book turns any printed page into a voice. Take a photo of a book page, and the AI reads it, explains it in easy words — in Urdu, Roman Urdu or English — and reads it aloud, so the page can be understood by listening, not only by reading. It was made first of all for mothers who cannot read their children's schoolbooks, for elders whose eyesight makes small print a struggle, and for children who have no teacher nearby to explain the lesson.

The idea: Bolti Duniya — a world where books speak

Loona Speaking Book carries a simple mission, called Bolti Duniya: every book should speak, in every language, for everyone who cannot read. Its maker is a school teacher who kept meeting the same three people. A mother who wanted to help with homework but could not read the textbook. A grandfather who had given up reading because the print had become too small. A child whose questions about the lesson had no one at home to answer them. A printed page is silent for all three of them — until it is photographed, and then it speaks.

Who it is for

For mothers. Photograph your child's book page, listen to the explanation, and then teach your child with confidence — even if you never had the chance to learn reading yourself.

For elders. Weak eyesight should not end a lifetime of reading. Photograph the page — a book, a letter, a medicine leaflet, a form — and simply listen.

For children and students. When there is no teacher at hand, the book itself becomes the teacher: the Explain mode retells the page in easy words, the way a kind teacher would, and the Read Only mode reads exactly what is written when you need the words themselves.

For language learners. Hear the same page in Urdu, Roman Urdu or simple English, and learn how the words sound as well as what they mean.

How it works, briefly

You take a photo of the page and choose a language and a reading mode. The photo is sent to an online AI service, which reads the page and prepares either the exact text or an easy explanation. The answer returns to this page as text, and your own phone or computer reads it aloud with its built-in voice. The full step-by-step account is on our How We Work page, and common questions are answered on the FAQ page.

An honest note about the AI

Because the reading is done by an AI service, your photo travels over the internet to that service and back — this tool cannot work fully offline, and we say so plainly. The explanation is a learning aid: it simplifies, and like any simplification it should be checked against the book itself for examination facts. Our Privacy Policy explains what happens to your photo.

Why this tool is free

We believe the door in front of a tool should never be the reason a person cannot use it. The people this page was built for are the very people who cannot manage sign-ups, trials and payment walls — so Loona Speaking Book has none: no account, no download, no fee. The site may show advertising in the future to cover its costs, but the tool itself stays free.

Who makes it

Loona Speaking Book is part of Loona Automation, built by Aurang Zeb Loona, a school teacher and AI teacher trainer in Jaranwala, Faisalabad, Punjab, Pakistan, who builds free, practical tools for everyday people and small businesses — especially in Pakistan, and for users everywhere in the world.
