The fastest way to reach the Loona Automation team is by email:

loonaautomation@gmail.com

We read every message and usually reply within 1–2 working days. Please mention Loona Speaking Book in your subject line so we can help you faster.

What to write to us about

A page that was read wrongly. If the AI misread a page or explained something incorrectly, tell us — reports like yours are how the tool gets better. If you can, name the book and the class (for example, “Class 3 General Knowledge”) and say what the page actually says.

Voice and language problems. If the voice reads Urdu words in an English accent, or the sound does not play at all, write to us. The voice comes from your own device, but we can usually tell you which setting or browser fixes it.

Ideas. A language you wish the book could speak, a kind of page it should handle better, a feature that would help your family — all are welcome.

Schools, teachers and organisations. If you want to use Loona Speaking Book with a class, a literacy group or a community library, we would love to hear how it goes and what your learners need.

Business and press. Enquiries about Loona Automation, our other free tools, or this project are welcome at the same address.

What to include, so we can help fast

Three small things save days of back-and-forth: your phone or computer model, the browser you used (for example Chrome), and one sentence on what happened versus what you expected. If a photo was involved, describe the page — its book, class and language — rather than sending the photograph itself.

What email cannot do

Please do not send book pages by email to be read for you — the tool on this site is the way to hear a page, and it is free. We also cannot recover passwords or accounts for other companies' services. For anything about Loona Speaking Book itself, though, if in doubt, just write — a real person reads it.
