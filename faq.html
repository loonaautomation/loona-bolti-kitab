What is Loona Speaking Book?

It is a free tool that makes a printed book page speak. You take a photo of the page, and an AI reads it and either reads the exact words aloud or explains the page in easy words first — in Urdu, Roman Urdu or English. It was built for people who cannot read the page themselves: mothers helping children with homework, elders with weak eyesight, and students with no teacher nearby.

How do I use it?

Four steps: (1) take or upload a clear photo of the book page; (2) choose the language — Urdu, Roman Urdu or English; (3) choose the reading mode — Explain & Read Aloud, or Read Only; (4) press “Start Reading the Book”. The answer appears as text on the page; press Listen to hear it, and Stop whenever you like. There is also a Demo button that shows a ready-made example without any photo.

What is the difference between Explain & Read Aloud and Read Only?

Read Only reads exactly what is written on the page, word for word. Explain & Read Aloud first retells the page in easy words, the way a teacher explains a lesson, and then reads that explanation aloud. Choose Read Only when the exact wording matters; choose Explain when understanding matters more than the original words.

Which languages can it speak?

Three: Urdu in its own script, Roman Urdu in easy words (Urdu written with English letters), and simple English. More languages are part of the long-term Bolti Duniya mission — every book speaking in every language.

Do I need an account, an app install or payment?

No. There is no sign-up, no fee and no watermark, and the tool works in your web browser. You may optionally install it as an app from the browser menu for one-tap access, but that is a convenience, not a requirement.

What happens to my photo?

Your photo is sent over the internet to the online AI service that reads the page, and the answer is sent back to your device. Loona Automation does not keep a copy of your photos or of the answers. The full details are in our Privacy Policy. Because the photo must travel to the AI, this tool needs an internet connection and does not work offline.

Why does the voice sound different on different phones?

The reading voice belongs to your own device: the page sends the text to your phone's or computer's built-in text-to-speech, so the voice, accent and quality depend on the voices installed on that device. For the best Urdu reading, choose the Urdu language option and use a device and browser (Chrome works well) that have an Urdu voice installed.

What kind of photo works best?

A flat, well-lit photo of a single printed page, taken straight-on so the text is sharp and not cut off at the edges. Printed schoolbooks and documents work best. Very faint print, heavy shadows, crumpled pages and handwriting can be misread — if the answer sounds wrong, retake the photo in better light before blaming the book.

Can it read a whole book?

One page at a time. Photograph each page you want to hear. For homework and daily reading this page-by-page rhythm is usually exactly what is needed — the page the child is studying today, the letter that arrived today.

Is Loona Speaking Book free?

Yes — completely. It is part of Loona Automation's free tools, built on the belief that a tool's door should never keep out the people who need it most. The site may show advertising in the future to cover its costs; the tool itself stays free.
