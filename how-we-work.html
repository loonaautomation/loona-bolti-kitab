How Loona Speaking Book Works

Loona Speaking Book · Loona Automation

Loona Speaking Book looks simple — a photo goes in, a voice comes out. This page explains, honestly and step by step, what actually happens in between, what we do with your photo, and where the limits of the tool are.

The journey of one page

1. You take the photo. The page image sits in the tool on your device while you choose the language (Urdu, Roman Urdu or English) and the reading mode. Nothing is sent anywhere until you press “Start Reading the Book”.

2. The photo travels to our AI engine. When you press the button, the photo is sent over the internet to an automation workflow (built on Make.com) connected to an AI reading model. This is the one step that cannot happen on your device alone — reading and explaining a page takes a large AI model, and that is also why the tool needs an internet connection.

3. The AI reads and prepares the answer. The model first recognises the text on the page. In Read Only mode it returns the words exactly as written. In Explain & Read Aloud mode it rewrites the page as an easy explanation in your chosen language — the way a teacher explains a lesson to a child, a mother or an elder.

4. The answer comes back as text. The prepared text returns to this page in your browser and is shown on screen, so it can be read silently too, copied, or read again later.

5. Your own device reads it aloud. When you press Listen, your phone or computer speaks the text with its own built-in text-to-speech voice. That is why the voice differs from device to device, why an Urdu voice must exist on the device for the best Urdu reading, and why the listening step itself works even on a slow connection — the voice is already yours.

What we do not do

We do not ask for an account, a name or a payment. Loona Automation does not keep a copy of your photos or of the answers the AI prepares, and we do not sell data — there is no data business here. This site itself sets no tracking cookies; the standard analytics and advertising information is covered in our Privacy Policy.

Why it is free

Loona Speaking Book is part of Loona Automation's family of free tools, built by a school teacher in Jaranwala, Faisalabad, Punjab, Pakistan under the mission name Bolti Duniya — every book should speak, in every language, for everyone who cannot read. The people this tool serves are the least able to pay for it, so it carries no price. Advertising may appear on the site in the future to cover running costs; the tool itself stays free.

The limits, honestly

A blurred, shadowed or crumpled page can be misread, and handwriting is harder for the AI than print. An explanation simplifies — that is its purpose — so for examination facts, confirm the key points in the book itself. And the tool reads one page at a time: it is a companion for the page in front of you, not a machine for swallowing whole libraries at once. When the AI gets a page wrong, telling us genuinely helps it get better.
