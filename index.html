For Mothers (Ammi ke liye)

For those who cannot read themselves — take a photo of your child's book, listen to the answer and teach your child. (Jo parh nahi saktin — bachay ki kitab ki photo lein, jawab sun kar bachay ko parha dein.)

For Elders (Buzurgon ke liye)
